# 0017. Does "No commentary. No spin." still describe the site?

- **Status:** open
- **Date raised:** 2026-10-09 · **Date decided:** —
- **Decided by:** —
- **Beads:** reportsthatmatter-pbkk

## Question

/about says "Just the source, made accessible" and "No commentary. No spin." Every report now has an editor's introduction, a "why it matters" line and editor's highlights, labelled as ours. Should the promise be reworded?

## Context

Found in the 2026-10-09 launch audit (docs/research/2026-10-09-launch-audit.md). The promise is about the report text, which we do not edit beyond the fidelity checks; the introductions and highlights are clearly labelled. A reader who meets "Editor's highlight" on a phone landing panel after reading "No commentary" may feel the site contradicts itself.

## Options

1. Leave it: the promise is about the report text.
2. Reword: "The report's own words, unedited. Our introductions and highlights are labelled as ours."
3. Drop the line.
