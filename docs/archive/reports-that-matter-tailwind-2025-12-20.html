Important public reports should not be trapped in unusable formats.

This treasure trove of high-quality information on some of the most important issues of our time should be accessible and usable. Reports That Matter makes it so.

What This Is

A stripped-back, web-native way to publish and read reports that matter.

We focus on presenting content clearly, removing unnecessary friction, and enabling use—not just availability. This project is both a platform in its own right and a gateway to the reports it hosts.

Core Focus

Accessibility. Durability. Clarity.

These reports deserve a publishing standard that matches their importance. We provide exactly that—nothing more, nothing less.

Why These Reports Matter

Reports produced through official inquiries are often exceptionally high quality: well-resourced, evidence-rich, carefully argued, and produced through rigorous processes.

Rigorous Investigation

These inquiries are conducted with extensive resources, expert testimony, and careful methodology.

Critical Issues

They typically arise in response to major events or failures—malfeasance, systemic failure, ecological crises.

Public Importance

These reports address issues of crucial public importance that deserve broad understanding and discussion.

The Problem

Despite their quality and importance, many reports are hard to find, hard to read, and hard to work with—often locked inside large, awkward PDFs.

This creates a quiet tragedy: extraordinary public knowledge exists, but much of it remains effectively inaccessible to those who could use it to inform understanding, drive discussion, and enable action.

What "Accessible" Means

Web-Native

Rather than PDF-bound, reports are published as web-native content that works across devices.

Searchable

Find specific information quickly with full-text search across all reports.

Browsable

Navigate reports intuitively with clear structure and logical organization.

Citable

Stable URLs enable precise citations and references to specific sections.

Easy to Reference

Extract quotes, data, and findings without struggling with PDFs.

Print-Friendly

Read offline or in print without losing readability or structure.
