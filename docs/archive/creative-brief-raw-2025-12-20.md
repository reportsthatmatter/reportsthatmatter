## Creative Brief

Official Inquiries & Public Reports Platform (Working Title)

### 1. Project Essence

This project exists to make **official inquiries, reports, and public documents legible, trustworthy, and durable on the web**.

It treats public reports not as marketing content or blogs, but as **civic artefacts**: documents that should be readable decades later, cited precisely, and encountered without distraction.

---

### 2. Core Purpose

To provide a clear, neutral, and technically robust way to publish and read official inquiries online—free from platform noise, editorial distortion, or unnecessary interface complexity.

---

### 3. Audience

Primary:

* Journalists
* Researchers
* Policy professionals
* Lawyers
* Civil servants
* Engaged citizens

Secondary:

* Archivists
* Librarians
* Transparency and accountability organisations
* Developers working on public-interest infrastructure

---

### 4. Brand Values

* Clarity over cleverness
* Trust over engagement
* Durability over novelty
* Public interest over platform incentives
* Precision over persuasion

---

### 5. Brand Personality

* Serious, but not austere
* Calm, restrained, institutional—but modern
* Neutral in tone, confident in purpose
* Technically competent without showing off

---

### 6. Visual Direction

* Minimalist
* Document-first
* High typographic standards
* Generous whitespace
* Print-friendly

---

### 7. Typography

* Serif or serif-adjacent for body text
* Simple sans-serif for navigation and metadata
* Strong hierarchy

---

### 8. Tone of Voice

* Plain
* Exact
* Measured
* Non-promotional

---

### 9. Naming Considerations

Names should suggest:

* public record
* inquiry
* evidence
* reports
* accountability

Avoid metaphors and playful language.

# Assistant — Simplified Core Creative Brief (Essence-Focused)

## Creative Brief

Official Inquiries & Public Reports Platform (Working Title)

### Core Idea

This project exists to make **important public information usable**.

Official inquiries and public reports often contain extraordinary insight, evidence, and learning—but today much of this material is effectively wasted: buried in PDFs, scattered across sites, or difficult to navigate and reference.

This platform presents that content **clearly, simply, and durably**, so it can actually be read, cited, discussed, and used to make a difference.

---

### What the Project Is

A stripped-back, web-native way to publish and read official inquiries and public reports.

It focuses on:

* accessibility
* durability
* clarity

It is both:

* a project in its own right, and
* a doorway into the reports it hosts.

---

### What Problem It Addresses

There is a quiet tragedy in how much high-quality public information exists—and how little impact it has.

Not because it is unimportant, but because it is:

* hard to access
* hard to read
* hard to work with

---

### Tone and Feel

* Warm, but restrained
* Serious, but human
* Simple, not spartan
* Calm, not institutional

Feels like a **well-kept reading room**.

---

### Design Principles

* Stripped back
* Document-first
* Minimal interface
* No performative design

# Assistant — Final Synthesised Creative Brief

## Creative Brief

Reports That Matter

### Core Idea

Reports That Matter exists to make important public information usable.

There is a treasure trove of unusually high-quality information on some of the most important issues of our time—contained in reports produced through official inquiries. This material deserves to be accessible and usable, so it can inform understanding, discussion, and action.

---

### What the Project Is

A stripped-back, web-native way to publish and read reports that matter.

It focuses on:

* presenting this content clearly
* removing unnecessary friction
* enabling use, not just availability

It is both:

* a project in its own right, and
* a lead-in to the reports it hosts.

---

### Why These Reports Matter

These reports are often unusually high quality and arise in response to major public issues—malfeasance, systemic failure, social and ecological crises.

---

### The Problem

Despite their quality and importance, many reports are hard to find, hard to read, and hard to work with—often locked inside large, awkward PDFs.

---

### What “Accessible” Means

Accessible here means:

* web-native rather than PDF-bound
* searchable
* browsable
* citable via stable URLs
* easy to navigate and reference

---

### Tone and Feel

Warm, restrained, serious, human.
A **well-kept reading room**.

---

### Design Principles

Stripped back.
Document-centred.
Minimal interface.

---

### Essence (Internal)

> This treasure trove of high-quality information on some of the most important issues of our time should be accessible and usable.
