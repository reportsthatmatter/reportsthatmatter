# Lessons: the running log

What we learned about getting better faster on this pipeline. Reportsthatmatter-38s.6 started it; every agent appends. It is a log, not a design: short entries, newest first inside each theme, each with the evidence (a bead or a doc) and what changed or is proposed. When a lesson becomes a check, a tool or a rule, link it here and move on; do not rewrite history, add a line.

**How to add one.** At the end of your task, after your final-report retro ("Getting better faster", agent protocol rule 12), append the entries that are worth keeping to the theme they belong to (or a new theme), in the form below, in the same PR as your work. One line per entry, never hard-wrapped. Skip what is only about your report; keep what would have helped the next agent on a different one. The integrator merges concurrent appends (it is an append-only file, so conflicts are trivial) and reads the log before each release.

`- **<lesson>** (YYYY-MM-DD, <bead or doc>, <who>) <evidence in a sentence>. <What changed, or the proposal>. [status: done in <PR> | proposed | open question]`

## Measuring

- **When the answer key runs out of errors, draw a new one where the errors are.** (2026-10-03, 38s.15, Opus) The dev set had 4 adjudicated page-break errors left at v0.21.0; 30 breaks per PDF-only report, drawn from the PDF's own page turns (15 random, 5 per confidence tier) and answered from the page images, held 28 (13 dev, 15 held-out halves) in about an hour of agent time. With ingest#57 they fall to 21, and the measure says where the rest are: notes or a credit left between the halves (9) and a capital, digit or full stop where the rules split (11). [status: done, `pnpm ingest referee draft`; docs/scoring.md "Mini-references"]
- **Adjudicate blind, and give the reader the image, not the text layer.** (2026-10-03, 38s.15) Readers saw the page crops and numbered lines but not the rules' call; on the two OCR reports the drawn "last line" was a footnote or a caption in 12 and 13 of 30 breaks, so the reader must be allowed to correct the lines, and the label must record the lines it judged. A second blind reader then re-read every break where reader and pipeline disagreed. [status: done, docs/design/learning/adjudicate-pagebreaks.md]
- **The confidence tier is not where the PDF-only errors are either.** (2026-10-03, 38s.15) At v0.21.0, 16 of the 28 mini-reference errors sat on high-confidence calls, mostly R1 joins the pass would have made had the halves been adjacent: a footnote left in the body between them (Challenger's scan, PSI, Deepwater) means the pass never sees the pair. Group errors by what stands between in *our* text before by rule outcome. [status: done, `referee eval --breakdown` classes "left between in our text" first]
- **A judge that cannot place a row hides errors.** (2026-10-03, 38s.15) `referee eval` left a break unjudged when the last line was short ("failed.481"), a heading lost its label in the output, or the continuation was displaced into a later block; a second pass and an `above` line judged 37 more rows on the old references (held-out 15 of 193 wrong became 20 of 228). Count unjudged rows next to the error rate, and keep the old reading (`--strict-judge`) to compare with recorded numbers. [status: done]
- **Block-style paragraphs make a page break undecidable from the page.** (2026-10-03, 38s.15) Deepwater sets paragraphs flush with a blank line between, so a full stop at a page foot followed by a flush line is a guess either way (8 of its 30 marked `sure: false`); a rule cannot learn from these, and neither can a referee shown the same lines. [status: open: record `sure` and report accuracy with and without]

- **A typography pass keeps the held-out reference honest where a tags pass would score itself.** (2026-10-03, a8l, Sonnet) Hillsborough's reference is the website HTML plus the PDF's tags, so reading the tags to find the PDF-filled stretches' headings would have made their score a tautology; the layout (size, weight, colour: what every PDF has) fixed 79 of 79 and moved the held-out PDF shadow's boundary F1 86.8 to 91.1 (recall 83.6 to 91.1) without being tuned on it: tuned and checked against golden pages read off the page images (pp.134, 378) and the oracle's headings-missed (144 to 70), and the score looked at once at the end. [status: done in ingest#60]
- **Read every filled stretch before and after a pass that adds blocks to a hybrid's PDF side: the fill has rules written for the blocks it used to see.** (2026-10-03, a8l, Sonnet) Cutting headings out of the shadow's paragraphs made `fillGaps` drop about a tenth of them (a heading whose words an edition heading also has, a 3-word heading whose words turn up in nearby prose, 13 copies of "Conclusion: what is added to public understanding" that the aligner leaves unmatched at a gap's edge), and no check counted it; a per-gap list of the layout's heading lines against the headings in `full.md` (79 of 79 in the end) found them, and `heading-repeated` caught the doubled copy. Fixed in ingest#60 (`layoutHeading`, `atEdge`). [status: done in ingest#60]
- **A gap-filler that exists on one path is a gap on the other.** (2026-10-03, d662) `fillPrintedGaps` numbered the unread pages between two read ones for the hybrid, but the PDF build marked only pages whose number it read, so 124 pages in 7 reports (709 blocks) were cited with the page before; `pnpm ingest anchors` found them, no existing check could. The PDF path now fills them, each marker at its page's first block (pages with no block get none, so markers never stack). The 15 unmarked pages in Leveson were not an in-step gap at all but a folio set beside a thumb-index letter, so check why a page lost its marker before filling it. [status: done in the d662 ingest PR]
- **Numbering that is not in step with the PDF cannot be inferred, only read.** (2026-10-03, d662) Hillsborough (printed 59 on PDF p.63, 61 on p.64), Challenger's OCR'd appendix pages and Jack Smith's appended letter stay unmarked because their neighbours' numbers do not run with the PDF page order; relaxing the in-step rule would invent numbers. Each needs its folio read (composite footers, OCR garble, letter running heads: ivg.3, uw50, ssfk). [status: open]
- **A reference edition that keeps note markers as words makes linking markers look like a WER regression.** (2026-10-03, izw) Columbia's held-out WER rose 0.44% → 0.65% when 179 bare markers became links; its reference reads "as if it were 29 the board", so every linked marker counts as a missing word, while boundary F1 and page-break joins rose. Strip bare marker digits from both sides before WER, or report WER with and without them. [status: proposed]
- **A page-keyed score can call a fix a regression.** (2026-10-03, y7ix, Opus agent) Rejoining Columbia's "At EI+790…" paragraph past a caption put its p.72 lines before `%%page 72%%`; `pnpm score --diff` listed 8 boundary rows on p.72 as correct to wrong, and WER rose 0.01 pt because the same words now sit in another order. Read flips against full.md before calling them losses; the marker format cannot sit mid-paragraph (i8en). [status: open question]
- **A page-level marker score near zero with a normal page WER is a label-format mismatch until proven otherwise.** (2026-10-03, j3fw) 9/11's page-marker P/R fell from 98% to 0.2% on the hybrid switch because Wikisource labels notes "4" and the hybrid's endnotes are `[^4-1]` (note-chapter); positions matched on 299 of 299 pages and the scorer required equal strings. Comparing the note number (not the chapter, which Wikisource gets wrong on pages 123 to 125) gave 98.5% / 99.1%. Print the first mismatching pair of any near-zero match rate before theorising. [status: done, `sameLabel` in `src/lib/score/pages.ts`]
- **A scorer's per-page worst list finds defects the sample checks do not.** (2026-10-03, 8x9n) Reading the worst pages by error count for 9/11 showed three consecutive pages of Appendix B with no text of their own and 800 of 2,296 page-level word errors: the long table's `%%page%%` stamps are stacked after its last row. Neither golden pages nor quality signals look inside a table. Bead filed. [status: open, 8x9n]
- **Record the number that describes the report, and label the other.** (2026-10-03, 79ze) For a hybrid, `scores.json` now records the PDF shadow as the report's score and the adapter-vs-reference-adapter run as `adapter` (agreement of two readers, shown with deltas but never flagged), and migrates a base file that recorded the adapter as the score without reading it as a regression. [status: done, `mergeScores` and `baselines` in `src/lib/score/headline.ts`]
- **A pipeline's marker count measures what it marked, never what it missed.** (2026-10-03, s24x, Opus) The independent check `pnpm ingest anchors` found 124 printed pages across 7 PDF-built reports that carry no `%%page%%` marker at all (Leveson's 15-page Summary of Recommendations, Deepwater's Appendix B, Challenger's `(N)` chapter openers, Hillsborough 21, Columbia 10, PSI's chart pages, Jack Smith's appended letter), leaving 709 blocks cited with the previous page; no golden page, oracle signal or quality budget saw it, and the hybrid path already fills such gaps (`fillPrintedGaps`) while the PDF path does not. [status: done in the s24x PR, budgets in `reports/anchor-budget.yaml`, in verify; fix bead filed]
- **An independent check is only independent if the page number is read independently too.** (2026-10-03, s24x) Reading each page's number off its own first and last lines, kept only where a neighbour runs in step, put every report on one generic reader (ivg.2's script needed a per-layout `printed()`); trying three label schemes and keeping the best measured b78.10's question: Philip Morris labels PDF pages, the other twelve printed numbers. [status: done]
- **Watch the checker fail on known-broken text before trusting its zero.** (2026-10-03, s24x) On the Saville PDF build it reproduced ivg.2's 12 misplaced paragraphs exactly and named the cause (pages 17, 49, 51 unmarked); a shingle allowed to cross a served block boundary had matched one coincidental word ("airports | The following" vs the PDF's "airports. The intelligence") and failed a correct 9/11 marker, and a first-shingle page start put a Leveson page 11,759 words away on a running head; both fixed before the numbers were believed. [status: done, tests in `tests/page-anchors.test.ts`]
- **A derived record must be rebuilt from the published base, not accumulated in the working tree.** (2026-10-03, s24x) `pnpm aliases generate` merged its output into the working tree's `published-ids.txt`, so a run against an intermediate re-ingest recorded ids that were never published; it now starts both files from `--old-ref`, and a second run on the final text undoes the first. [status: done in the s24x PR]

- **Measure a decider's ceiling and floor before paying for it.** (2026-10-03, 38s.11, docs/design/2026-10-03-pagebreak-referee.md) An oracle referee (the adjudicated truth where known) and an inverting one (always against the rules), run through the same path as the real model, showed that the rules' ambiguous page-break calls held none of the 26 adjudicated errors: a perfect LLM asked only about them would have changed nothing. Widening to medium-confidence calls raised the ceiling to 3 of 4 dev errors and 1 of 22 held-out. [status: done, `pnpm ingest referee eval --answers oracle|invert`]
- **"Ambiguous" by threshold is not where the errors are; rate confidence by measured error rate per rule outcome.** (2026-10-03, 38s.11) The page-break errors sat on calls the rules made confidently: a flush first line after a full stop on a ragged page (2 of 2 dev adjudicated wrong) and a font change caused by a footnote or caption between the halves (6 of 25 held-out). Tabulate each rule outcome against the labels before choosing what to refer. [status: done, `pnpm ingest referee eval --breakdown`]
- **Judge a decision from the pipeline's own blocks when the scorer is too slow or measures something else.** (2026-10-03, 38s.11) Reading which side of each adjudicated break the two printed lines landed on takes about 5 s a report with no prerender and works on a clean-edition report's PDF shadow; it cannot read a page whose columns were interleaved (Duelfer: 1 error against `pnpm score`'s 5), so report it beside the scorer, not instead of it. [status: done, `pnpm ingest referee eval`]
- **A wholly missing page is invisible to an edition's fidelity check.** (2026-10-03, ivg.3, gap-fill agent) `PDF text not in the edition` needs an aligned word on the page, so the 30-odd Hillsborough pages the website archive never captured raised no suspect at all; the served text would simply have lost 43,000 words. The adapter now declares its gaps and `fillGaps` fills them from the PDF shadow. [status: proposed, a signal for PDF pages with no edition word and no declared gap]
- **Score the shadow against the reference, not only the served text.** (2026-10-03, ivg.3) Hillsborough's held-out reference is itself built from the same website HTML, so the hybrid's +11 F1 is partly circular; the PDF shadow against the reference (F1 76.1 → 77.1, joins 80.6% → 97.0%, adjudicated 7/30 → 0/30) is the number a pass can be judged by. Writing the shadow's markdown to the site copy and running `pnpm score` is a manual step. [status: proposed, `pnpm score <id> --shadow-ref`; see 79ze]
- **Check page anchors with code that does not share the aligner.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) The hybrid's page stamps come from the same alignment that fills `pages.anchored`, so that number cannot fail; locating each paragraph's opening words in raw `pdftotext` page text and reading the page off its own running head found 12 of 2,079 paragraphs on the wrong page in the PDF build (pages 49 and 51 had no number at all) and 0 of 1,652 in the hybrid. [status: proposed, `pnpm ingest anchors <id>`; `docs/design/reference-editions/tools/anchors.py`]
- **When a report moves to its clean edition, its dev-set number changes meaning.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) `pnpm score uk-saville-inquiry` now scores the edition adapter against the reference adapter that reads the same HTML (boundary P 100%, R 76.6%: it joins a boxed extract's paragraphs into one quotation), while the PDF pipeline's score (89.4% F1 against the served text) is `--shadow`; `docs/scores.json` would record the first as the report's score and look like a 10-point regression. Record the shadow run for a hybrid report and the adapter agreement as its own line. [status: proposed, bead filed]
- **Two independent readers of one HTML are a cheap test of an adapter.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) The scorer's Python reference adapter and the report's TypeScript adapter agree on 1,322 of 1,322 paragraphs, 866 quotations, 1,439 of 1,442 markers and 0.06% of words; the disagreements were all design choices (one quotation per box, contents entries). Keep `reference.py` as the second reader and read its confusion matrix before trusting an adapter.
- **A number beats an anecdote, but only a number on the decision you changed.** (2026-10-02, 38s.10) The layout-gated page-break pass was judged by page-break join accuracy on adjudicated breaks, not by F1 over all boundaries (which moves by tenths because page breaks are 3% of line breaks). Report the metric of the decision, and the all-rows metric beside it. [status: `pnpm score` prints both and `docs/scores.json` records them]
- **Record the headline numbers where a diff can see them.** (2026-10-02, 38s.6 merger retro) Two integrators could not check a PR's join-accuracy claim because the numbers lived in PR prose. [status: done, `docs/scores.json` with previous value and delta; `pnpm scorecard` diffs it against main]
- **Gate scoring on the reference's own error rate.** (2026-10-02, 38s.12) Hillsborough's tag reference was wrong at 7.1% of page breaks and Chilcot's at 18.2% (40% wrong or missing); a score against a noisy reference measures the reference. Adjudicate 30 page breaks per reference before trusting it; fixing the reference (hybrid HTML plus tags) took Hillsborough to 0.0% and Chilcot to 4.5%. [status: `pnpm score` warns when error plus no-answer share exceeds 5% + 10%]
- **Labels need a confidence, and some rows must be dropped.** (2026-10-02, 38s.12) A reference that splits in lower case after an unfinished sentence is almost always a page-run artefact (a caption between paragraph halves); the label-flag filter caught 5 of the 6 wrong labels of the old references. Train and score on `high` and `adjudicated` rows, or report both.
- **Say how the sample was chosen.** (2026-10-02, 38s.4 golden pages, quality-harness.md) Golden pages are chosen from the hard cases, so they over-represent defects and say nothing about the rate across a report; the adjudicated random stratum is the unbiased sample. Never quote a golden-page pass rate as a defect rate.
- **A shared blind spot between producer and checker hides defects.** (2026-09-28, b78.1 "missing id") `corpus check` fingerprints the ids that the same functions produce, so 185 non-ASCII ids absent from the paragraph index passed every gate until an agent writing an introduction tripped on one. The checker must read the shipped artifact with independent code.
- **Alignment must be global and monotonic.** (2026-10-02, 38s.1) A first-anchor aligner mis-anchored a repeated-boilerplate paragraph at 27%; unique 7-gram anchors plus a longest increasing subsequence fixed it.
- **Oracle signals need a precision before they get a budget.** (2026-10-02, b78.5) `paragraphs-merged` is 10% precise because it counts every quotation paragraph and list item as a missed split; `markers-unlinked` is 59% until three oracle gaps are fixed. A ratchet on a noisy signal ratchets noise.

- **A text-matching contract must be written against the text's typography, not its bytes.** (2026-10-03, qgf7) `anchor.js` matched by exact characters, so swapping a report's source (PDF text layer to clean HTML) turned straight quotes into curly ones and would have silently orphaned every `?h=` link and saved mark with an apostrophe, though paragraph ids were unchanged. Anchors now fold quotes, dashes, ellipses, nbsp, ligatures and soft hyphens on both sides and map offsets back to the printed text. [status: done in the qgf7 PR]
- **Check stored user data against the new text before a source swap, not after.** (2026-10-03, qgf7) A read-only D1 query plus a locate() simulation over the PR's rendered text showed 1 of 12 Saville marks (the O'Donnell sentence) would have failed to anchor; nothing in `verify` or `corpus check` replays stored marks against a candidate text. [status: done in the p4h6 PR, `pnpm marks check`, release checklist step 7]
- **An alias contract has several consumers; test each one.** (2026-10-03, p4h6) `?p=` follows `aliases.yaml`, so aliases looked complete, but `social-proof.js` looks marks up by `getElementById(storedId)` and `/marks` returns stored ids untouched; replaying production marks found one (Jack Smith, 1 reader) already stranded this way with the alias in place. `pnpm marks check` fails an alias-only mark on purpose and passes an alias-only link. [status: bug filed, see the bead discovered from p4h6]
- **Replay what users stored with the product's own matcher, per kind of reference.** (2026-10-03, p4h6) Marks, queued `?h=` links and editorial quotes need different things from a new text (a link survives an id move and a words-moved-paragraph fallback; a mark and an editorial id do not), so one pass/fail per reference would have been wrong in both directions. `src/lib/marks-check.ts` calls `locate`, `placeQuote` and `followAlias` and decides failure per kind. Current corpus: 163 marks (plus one smoke-test row for an unregistered report), 46 queued links, 516 editorial references, 1 failure. [status: done in the p4h6 PR]

## Dev and held-out discipline

- **A held-out look should be the last run, and only one.** (2026-10-03, y7ix, Opus agent) The task listed the 21 held-out errors up front (from 38s.11's breakdown), and the final `pnpm ingest try` over every report is also the held-out read; it found a Leveson (PDF-only) regression whose guard then went in after the look. Keep a dev-plus-PDF-only try loop (`ingest try <ids>`), and name the held-out reports only in the final run. [status: proposed, `ingest try --exclude-held-out`]
- **The PDF-only reports are the best development set for page-break work.** (2026-10-03, y7ix, Opus agent) The dev set had 4 adjudicated page-break errors left, none of the upstream shape; Deepwater, Leveson, Lehman and Challenger had dozens (continuations glued to captions, credits, footnotes and running heads), each readable against the page. Tune there, check dev for regressions, read held-out once. [status: done in ingest#57]
- **Develop on the development set, read the held-out set once.** (2026-10-02, 38s.8) The page-break rules were written from reading development errors and scored once on held-out reports from other publishers; the gain held (80.8% to 88.9%) but the held-out reports were also read afterwards to explain numbers, which is the first step to tuning on them. [status: `reports/score-sets.yaml` lists the sets; `pnpm score` reports them separately and warns when a held-out score falls; the release checklist asks whether a pass was tuned on held-out]
- **A learned model that does not transfer is information.** (2026-10-02, 38s.8) A block-type tree trained across reports scored 64-74% leave-one-report-out against the pipeline's 87-98%, because each publisher marks a quotation differently; the right form was per-report typography declared in `ingest.ts`, not a shared model. Ship rules, use trees to propose them.
- **A reference artefact looks like a regression.** (2026-10-02, 38s.9) `quoteListRunOns` took Saville's join accuracy from 92.2% to 89.7% because the reference splits boxed extracts at the PDF's page breaks; the eleven correct joins scored as missed splits. Read the diff against the page before reverting a pass, and say so in the PR.

## Tools that would have saved time

- **A pass the installed library does not know is ignored without a word, and a before/after that links the library into one of its two consumers measures nothing.** (2026-10-03, a8l) `pnpm ingest run` imports the report's `ingest.ts` (which imports `@rtm/ingest` from the report repo's `node_modules`) but runs the site's copy of the pipeline: with only the report linked to the unreleased ingest the new pass was simply not there and `full.md` came out byte-identical, and every pnpm script's preflight reinstalls the pinned copy over a symlink (the ivg.3 lesson again, third time). Proposal: `resolvePasses` should throw on a pass name it does not implement, and `pnpm ingest try` already links both, so use it, or a `pnpm ingest link ../ingest-x` that links both and survives preflight. [status: proposed]
- **Build LLM-in-the-loop steps so they can be tested without a key.** (2026-10-03, 38s.11) The referee's library half takes a transport and has no SDK; the host's `--fake split`, `--record` and `--replay` exercised the whole fill, write and build path on Philip Morris with no API key, and the committed cache keeps every build offline and deterministic. The live measurement is a command, not a rewrite. [status: done in the 38s.11 PRs]
- **A one-command measurement loop for hybrid work.** (2026-10-03, ivg.3) Each iteration needed: re-ingest in the report worktree, copy full.md into the site worktree, re-link `@rtm/ingest` (pnpm's pre-run install undoes a symlink), prerender, verify (oracle and golden), quality report, score, and a hand-made shadow score; plus a throwaway script to print `EditionReport` and its gaps. Piping that script into `head` once killed it before it restored the site's full.md. [status: proposed, `pnpm ingest try` with `--keep` covers part; `pnpm ingest run` should print the edition report]
- **Fetch every Wayback capture date, not only the latest.** (2026-10-03, ivg.3) 15 of 45 Hillsborough pages returned 503 from their latest captures (the site was dying) and 200 from 2012-2015 captures listed by the CDX API; the reference had been built without them and called chapters 4, 7, 8, 9 "missing" when the Foreword, the whole Report summary, Part 3, the appendices and 9 chapter pages were there. [status: proposed, report-source skill: query CDX with `filter=statuscode:200` and try the oldest first]
- **`pnpm ingest page --fixture` writes one page's XML without the fontspecs defined on earlier pages.** (2026-10-03, b89) A multi-page test fixture built from two such files parsed every font as empty and read no raised digits; copying the fontspecs in from the cached document XML fixed it. Make `--fixture` include every fontspec the page uses. [status: proposed]
- **A pre-merge block dump and a before/after paragraph diff were the whole loop.** (2026-10-03, y7ix, Opus agent) A temporary env hook in `mergeAcrossPages` writing the blocks it receives (kind, page, off-face, the layout pair and decision) plus an off switch for the new rule gave a per-report before/after diff in seconds, against 10 minutes for `pnpm ingest try`. Proposal: `pnpm ingest blocks <id> --stage merge` and `ingest try --fast` (no prerender, no score, rendered text only). [status: proposed]
- **Make derived data explicit.** (2026-10-03, i8en) Paragraph ids, per-block source and the page-label scheme exist only inside a function or in memory, so answering "which edition did this paragraph come from" or "what is this id" means re-running the pipeline. A generated per-block ledger (`blocks.jsonl`) beside `full.md` would let checks and outsiders read them directly. [status: proposed, decision 0002]
- **`pnpm ingest run` should print the edition report.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) Aligned words, out-of-vocabulary words, pages anchored by their own words, repairs and disagreements are in `IngestResult.edition` but the CLI prints none of it, so each hybrid report needs a throwaway script to see its own fidelity. [status: proposed, with `pnpm ingest anchors <id>`]
- **Run `pnpm aliases generate` once, against the final text.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) It adds every id of the new text to `published-ids.txt`; run against an intermediate re-ingest it recorded eight ids that were never published and `pnpm aliases check` failed on them. Restore both files from `origin/main` and generate again. [status: proposed, generate should record only ids of the text it is told is final, or take `--dry-run`]
- **Hybrid work needs the report, the site and the ingest linked, and the names have to agree.** (2026-10-03, ivg.2) Three worktrees, a link override in two `node_modules`, a manifest `dir` edit and an `RTM_REPORT_DIRS` directory keyed by the repo's basename (so a renamed worktree is silently not read). [status: bead 1w3, `pnpm ingest try`]
- **Concurrent sessions overwrite each other's caches.** (2026-10-02, b78.9 retro, bead 14su) `pnpm ingest verify` writes `.cache/oracle.json` in the shared report repo. Key caches by worktree and ingest version. [status: proposed]
- **Testing an unreleased ingest takes too many manual steps.** (2026-10-02, 38s.9 retro, bead 1w3) A site-only link fails with "does not provide an export" because report repos import `@rtm/ingest` from their own node_modules. Built `pnpm ingest try <branch|path> [ids]` (per-trial report worktrees, join/move list, quality and score deltas, restores links) and `pnpm score --diff <outA> <outB>`. [status: PR open, bead 1w3]
- **Learn the publish quota before uploading, not from a failed publish.** (2026-10-02, h6b, v0.18.0 integrator) D1's daily row-write limit blocked eight reports for a day and the only probe was a failed full publish. [status: proposed, `pnpm publish-report --preflight`]
- **Build the inspection kit with the check.** (2026-10-02, b78.5 retro) `pnpm ingest page <id> <vol> <pdfPage>` (layout lines beside the blocks made) and `--fixture` turned golden pages from a week of work into a day. Every new check ships with the command that shows a human what it saw.
- **A page needs its full checked text, not just its structure.** (2026-10-03, zphc retro, 7d4y) Wikisource's proofread pages give page-level word and marker references for 9/11 and the Chilcot executive summary (WER 0.79% and 0.60%); golden pages should carry checked text so every report, scans included, gets a word score. - **Peer noise fails the gate.** (2026-10-02, b78.2) `verify.sh`'s aggregate step pulls a peer's dirty report repo and trips editorial, corpus and quality checks for reports you do not own. Check `git -C ../<repo> status` first; do not edit what you do not own.
- **Run the sourcing checklist's mechanical half by command.** (2026-10-03, ifb5 retro, design §9) Walking Duelfer II-III through stage 1 by hand, `pdfinfo -box`, `pdftotext -bbox` and a Wayback CDX listing found three facts that would otherwise have surfaced as stage 4 defects or wrong decisions, in minutes. [status: proposed, `pnpm source probe` ifb5.10]
