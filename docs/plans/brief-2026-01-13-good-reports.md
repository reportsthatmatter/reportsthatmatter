**Why this report:**

- Highly controversial and politically charged
- Directly relevant to Trump's current presidency
- Volume 2 still being fought over in courts—ongoing news hook
- Detailed, quotable, evidence-based

## Selection Criteria

Reports were evaluated based on:
1. **Current news relevance** — Will people care right now?
2. **Controversy potential** — Will it spark engagement on Twitter/X?
3. **Quality of source document** — Is it linkable and quotable?
4. **Audience fit** — Politically engaged Twitter users interested in primary sources
