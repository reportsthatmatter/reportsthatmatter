  1. The relationship between TIRC/CTR and Hill & Knowlton remained close for many years. Because TIRC had no headquarters and no staff when it was formed, Hill & Knowlton provided a working staff and temporary office space and assigned one of its experienced executives, Wilson Hoyt, to serve as Executive Secretary for the TIRC. In early 1956, the TIRC Executive Committee approved the relocation of TIRC's offices to the building where Hill & Knowlton's offices were located. At their January 29, 1964 meeting, the TIRC Executive Committee agreed to immediately transfer seven Hill & Knowlton employees, including Hoyt, to TIRC. TLT0902041- 2064 (US 88364); 93218985-8986 (US 21116); TLT0900114-0115 (US 88402); CTRMN003816- 3835 at 3825 (US 21147).
