Engineering is designing tools to separate the pieces. The prints should be released tomorrow. 2. The inert segments are at T-24 and are undergoing inspection. 3. The hot flow test rig i s in design, which is proving to be difficult. Engineering is planning release of these prints Wednesday or Thursday. 4.
