Mr. Trump's Pressure on the Vice President

¶Mr. Trump wanted no one else speaking with Vice President Pence because he and co-conspirators were already implementing a secret plan to use Mr. Pence's ministerial role as President of the Senate to Mr. Trump's advantage. Co-Conspirator 2, with assistance from Co-Conspirators 5 and 6, spearheaded the execution of a strategy-which Co-Conspirator 2 had recently conceded was not supported by the Constitution or federal law 80-for Mr. Pence to decline to count the legitimate electoral certificates in the targeted states where Mr. Trump's electors had signed fraudulent ones.81 See ECF No. 252 at 65 & n.352; SCO-12184337, SCO-12184338 (Email and memo from Co-Conspirator 2 to Co-Conspirator 5 and Co-Conspirator 6 12/23/2020); SCO-12101300, SCO-12101301 (Email and memo from Co-Conspirator 2 to Co-Conspirator 6 01/03/2021). See, e.g., ECF No. 1 at~ 90; see ECF No. 252 at 62-74; see, e.g., SCO-00014655 at 157-182; SCO-00014442 at 29-43 (Pence, So Help Me God pp. 441-455); SCO-04982306 (Handwritten notes 12/25/2020); SCO-04982309 In the weeks before the certification, Mr. Trump began pressuring Mr. Pence to cooperate, both directly and by mobilizing Mr. Trump's supporters.
